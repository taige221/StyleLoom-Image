# 01 自然纪实摄影

由外层 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) 选择后按需读取；共享内容约束、图片类型、参考图处理、工具调用与留档规则由外层负责。

## Style prompt (English, primary)

Render the specified content as natural documentary photography. Keep shapes, scale, and materials believable, including subtle surface irregularities. Lighting must follow the specified time, weather, and light sources. Set focus and depth of field so the subjects, actions, and key relationships remain readable. Natural materials, plausible illumination, and restrained color grading should create a sense of being present at the scene. Avoid excessive skin smoothing, sharpening, or conspicuous filters.

## 中文参照（不用于拼接）

将指定内容呈现为自然纪实摄影观感。形态、尺度和材质可信，保留自然的表面起伏与细微不均匀性。受光符合指定的时间、天气和光源条件；焦点与清晰范围保证主体、动作和关键关系可读。以自然材质、合理光照和克制调色共同形成现场感，避免过度磨皮、锐化与滤镜化处理。

## 核心关系

自然材质、合理受光和服务于内容的焦点共同形成现场感。
