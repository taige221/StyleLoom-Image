## Style prompt (English, primary)

Render the specified content as layered papercut relief made from thin colored-paper shapes. Translate the given forms into nearly planar cut sheets; build features and parts by overlapping distinct paper layers. Make cut contours, thin paper cross-sections, slight offsets, and short shadows between layers visibly explain shape and shallow depth. Let the paper's own color and restrained grain distinguish the layers. Interpret the scene's viewing geometry through overlapping sheets rather than reconstructing a fully enclosed 3D model. Avoid folded paper dolls, origami, box-like objects, thick solid sculpture, and ordinary volume models with a paper texture applied. Keep the specified objects and framing without adding craft tools, decorative borders, frames, or unrelated paper ornaments.

## 中文参照（不用于拼接）

将指定内容表现为薄彩纸层叠形成的剪纸浅浮雕。既定形体转为近似平面的剪裁层片，通过不同薄纸层的遮挡叠合建立五官和部件。以可见剪裁轮廓、薄纸断面、轻微层间错位及短投影解释造型和浅层深度，纸张固有色与克制纸纹用于分层。场景视角通过纸层投影表达，不重建封闭实体三维模型。避免折叠纸偶、折纸、纸盒状物体、厚实体雕塑及仅贴纸纹的普通体积模型；保留指定物件与取景，不新增工具、装饰边框、相框或无关纸艺元素。

## 核心关系

薄平面纸片的剪裁轮廓、叠合遮挡和层间短阴影共同建立浅浮雕空间。

## 使用边界

- 深度主要来自层片关系，不将纸雕改成折叠纸偶、折纸或普通实体三维模型。
- 背景仅处理任务已有内容，不自动加入贺卡边框或节庆图案。
