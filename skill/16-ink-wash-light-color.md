# 16 水墨淡彩写意

由外层 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) 选择后按需读取；共享内容约束、图片类型、参考图处理、工具调用与留档规则由外层负责。

## Style prompt (English, primary)

Render the specified content as an expressive ink-wash painting with light color. Distill forms through brush-tip articulation, changing ink density, wet and dry marks, and simplified lines and washes. Use restrained color for necessary local colors and environmental relationships. Balance dark ink with sparse pale strokes, varying edge definition and negative space to establish depth. Keep identifying features and essential actions clear. Simplify only the specified background; do not add traditional landscapes, period clothing, inscriptions, or seals. Retain an expressive rendering of the given objects rather than converting them into unrelated abstract gestures.

## 中文参照（不用于拼接）

将指定内容表现为水墨淡彩写意画。以笔锋、墨色浓淡、干湿变化和概括线面提炼形体，淡彩补充必要颜色及环境关系。浓墨与疏淡笔触相互呼应，通过虚实、留白和笔势建立层次；主体辨识点与关键动作保持清楚。背景按既定内容概括，不自动添加传统山水、题款或印章。

## 核心关系

浓淡干湿、笔锋和虚实关系共同提炼既定形体。

## 使用边界

- 水墨画法可以表现现代生活，不自动改成古代服装或传统山水。
- 与 30 区分：以既定对象的写意表达为主，不默认转为非具象笔势。
