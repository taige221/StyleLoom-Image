## Style prompt (English, primary)

Render the specified content as an oil-pastel drawing. Organize forms with broad, forceful color marks, retaining waxy coverage, coarse grain, and rubbed or layered traces. Use overlapping color masses, directional strokes, and selective lines to establish structure. Allow naturally uneven edges while preserving important identifying features. Keep a strong handmade character; do not smooth away the marks or replace them with fine colored-pencil hatching. Do not automatically infantilize people or add playful decorations.

## 中文参照（不用于拼接）

将指定内容表现为油画棒手绘。采用较宽、有力度的色痕组织形体，保留蜡质覆盖感、粗颗粒和叠色擦痕。以色块交叠、笔触方向与局部线条共同塑造结构，边缘允许自然不齐，但关键特征保持可辨。画面具有鲜明的手绘力度，避免把笔触全部磨平或处理成细密彩铅排线。

## 核心关系

宽厚色痕、蜡质覆盖和方向明确的叠色共同建立形体与手绘力度。

## 使用边界

- 与 13 区分：核心是粗颗粒与较宽色痕，不是细密彩铅排线。
- 不自动幼化人物或增加童趣装饰物。
