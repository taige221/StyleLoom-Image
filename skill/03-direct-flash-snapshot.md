# 03 直闪快照

由外层 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) 选择后按需读取；共享内容约束、图片类型、参考图处理、工具调用与留档规则由外层负责。

## Style prompt (English, primary)

Render the specified content as a direct-flash photographic snapshot. Use a small, hard flash close to the camera axis as the main instantaneous illumination, directed at the subject rather than bounced or diffused through a softbox. Show distinct flash highlights on appropriate surfaces and clearly edged cast shadows on existing surfaces behind the subject, consistent with the near-camera light direction and actual distances. Balance the flash-lit foreground with believable, still-readable ambient exposure. Preserve natural skin, fabric, and surface detail, with restrained retouching and a candid snapshot character. Direct highlights, hard cast shadows, and ambient exposure must work together. Avoid a result dominated by soft window light, shadowless fill, or cinematic lighting. Preserve the specified time and setting; do not turn daylight into night, uniformly black out the background, or add surfaces or objects just to receive shadows.

## 中文参照（不用于拼接）

将指定内容呈现为直闪摄影快照。以靠近镜头轴线的小面积硬闪光作为主要瞬时照明，直接照向主体，不经柔光箱或跳闪。适当表面出现鲜明闪光高光，主体在后方既有表面留下边缘清楚、方向与距离可信的投影。闪光照亮的前景与仍可读的环境曝光保持平衡，保留自然皮肤、织物和表面细节，以克制修饰形成抓拍感。避免侧窗柔光主导、无投影补光或电影布光；保留指定时间与环境，不把白天改为夜晚、不统一压黑背景，也不为投影新增物件或承影面。

## 核心关系

近镜头硬闪高光、清晰投影和可信环境曝光共同形成直闪快照感。

## 使用边界

- 不自动将白天改成夜晚，不增加派对、人物或其他场景内容。
- 若用户锁定原图光照且仅要求局部修改，不用本预设覆盖保留要求。
